pdnsim DC correlation kit - design DC-A rev C - variant DC-A@JLCPCB - fabrication notes

These boards are 4-wire test coupons: their copper is measured to about 0.1 %. Build them exactly as drawn. Nothing on them is a measurement until they are measured.

JLCPCB disregards English text inside the upload zip: the order-form settings and the PCB Remark below are what counts (kit/correlation/ORDERING.md lists every field). This file records what was asked for.

Order form:
* Base material FR-4; layers 2; dimensions 99.06 x 99.06 mm; PCB qty 5; PCB thickness 1.6 mm.
* Outer copper weight: 1 oz.
* Surface finish: ENIG. Every pad is a spring-probe contact area, which JLCPCB's form excludes for OSP; NOT HASL (solder in open via barrels changes their resistance).
* Solder mask colour: green; silkscreen white.
* Via covering: tented.
* Mark on PCB: 2D barcode -> Number only (serial number), Specify Position: the 2 x 10 mm solid silkscreen square at the top edge of the front; prefix A1-, no unique number, start 0001. No order number (JLCPCB no longer prints one; the boards carry no 'JLCJLCJLCJLC').
* Electrical test: flying probe (the free random test is enough).
* Confirm Production File: yes (compare the production Gerbers with these: copper unchanged, via lands 0.75 and 1.00 mm as drawn (reject an annular-ring enlargement), no teardrops or thieving, every via tented).

PCB Remark:
Test coupon for 4-wire resistance measurement: do not modify copper (no teardrops, no copper thieving, do not fill or merge gaps, keep every via pad at its drawn diameter and every inner-layer pad removal as drawn). Tent all vias on both sides (no plugging). Serial number in the 2x10 mm silkscreen square.

Board facts:
* Board: FR-4, 2 copper layers, 99.06 x 99.06 mm, 1.60 mm nominal (1.6 mm ordered), quantity 5.
* Copper (finished, nominal): 35 / 35 um top to bottom (1oz outer).
* Plated holes: every plated hole is a VIA; the drill file's sizes (0.30 and 0.50 mm) are finished hole sizes, the fab's usual convention: do not change them. Via lands 0.75 and 1.00 mm as drawn (no annular-ring enlargement). Nominal barrel plating 18 um (the fab's published value). The four 3.2 mm holes are unplated (NPTH) mounting holes with no copper around them.
* Every via is covered by solder mask on both sides in the Gerbers: TENTED, not plugged, not filled.
* Do NOT modify copper: no teardrops, no copper thieving (each layer carries its own floating balancing copper), no filling or merging of copper gaps (same-net gaps of 0.25 mm or more are intentional), no added or removed via pads.
